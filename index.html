Our Journey

Since 1990, Tama Group has been at the forefront of healthcare innovation in Egypt. What started as a vision to create a specialized health division has blossomed into a comprehensive healthcare solutions provider.

Our strategy of horizontal diversification has led to a broad range of activities, including importation, distribution, export, joint manufacturing, and knowledge transfer. Today, we stand as a beacon of excellence in the healthcare sector.
